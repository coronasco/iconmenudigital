# Sir George / Master Ciok

Researched 2026-11-17. All 14 products are listed in the current official collection:
https://www.masterciok.it/it/prodotti/bevande-calde/te-e-tisane/sir-george/referenze/products.1.3.871.sp.uw

Each entry in `public/tea-data.js` records its individual official product URL and
original photograph URL. The PNG files are unmodified manufacturer product photos,
downloaded with `scripts/download-teas.mjs`, not AI-generated or stock substitutes.

The owner confirmed all teas at EUR 3.00. These are bar serving prices, not the
manufacturer's retail/package prices.

Ingredient wording is based on the individual product descriptions. Only Summer
Solstice, Bonne Nuit, Dopo Pasto and Ventre Plat publish an explicit ingredient list
on those pages; their lists are condensed without changing ingredients. Other
entries explicitly say "Ingredienti principali", not a complete ingredient list.
Oro Vivace says "Note aromatiche": the manufacturer describes a Manuka honey flavor
without identifying whether it is honey or flavoring.

Frutti di Bosco's named main ingredients are also published in the official
collection's indexed product description (M2170): elderberry, hibiscus, apple,
blackcurrant, blackberry, strawberry and raspberry. Its newer individual page
describes only a forest-fruit blend; the menu does not claim an exhaustive formula.

The menu refers customers to staff for current full composition, allergens and
availability. No medical, detox, weight-loss or caffeine-free claims are copied.
